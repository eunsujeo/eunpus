## (1) Super Validator 규모

source: canton.foundation 홈

- **54 Super Validator Nodes** 가 Global Synchronizer 를 공동 운영(decentralized service).
- SV = "known organizations, including household names in financial services and fintech"(WebSearch 요약).
- Synchronizer(보라)·Super Validator(회색)는 **우리가 운영하지 않는 공용 글로벌 계층** — SV 들이 공동 운영.

## (2) 거버넌스

source: canton.foundation · press/2025 (Goldman 등 합류)

- 거버넌스 = **Canton Foundation**(구 **Global Synchronizer Foundation, GSF**; 2025-09-22 개명).
- GSF = "independent U.S.-based entity composed of forward-thinking market participants … governed transparently in a decentralized manner with organizational neutrality".
- 출범 시 **Linux Foundation 파트너십**(canton.network/docs 근거, Stage 54·62). "The Foundation ensures fair governance and oversight of the Global Synchronizer."
