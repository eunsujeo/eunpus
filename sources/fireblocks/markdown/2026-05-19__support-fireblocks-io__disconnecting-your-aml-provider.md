        Disconnecting your AML provider

            Note
            The AML integration is a premium feature that requires an additional purchase. Reach
            out to your Customer Success Manager if you do not have an agreement with an AML
            provider or you need help enabling the feature.

        You can disconnect your Anti-Money Laundering (AML) provider from your workspace.
        Transactions initiated in your workspace will no longer be sent to your AML provider for
        transaction screening. You can reconnect your AML Provider account at any time.
        To disconnect your AML provider, contact Fireblocks Support.
