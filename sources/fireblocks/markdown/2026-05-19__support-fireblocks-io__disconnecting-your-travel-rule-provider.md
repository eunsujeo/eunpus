        Disconnecting your Travel Rule
        provider

            Note
            The Travel Rule integration is a premium, opt-in feature that requires an additional
            purchase. Contact your Customer Success Manager for more information.

        You can disconnect your Travel Rule provider from your workspace. Transactions initiated in
        your workspace will no longer be sent to Notabene for Travel Rule compliance. You can
        reconnect your Notabene account at any time.
        To disconnect your Travel Rule provider, contact Fireblocks Support.
