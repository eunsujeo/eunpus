# Linked users - Fireblocks mobile app

*Updated 1 year ago* (as of 2026-05-18 capture)

<!-- page: 1 -->

The Fireblocks mobile app can link multiple users and workspaces. Within the app, the Linked Users screen allows you to view, add and delete users and workspaces linked with the device.

## View all linked users

1. Open the Fireblocks app on your mobile device.
2. Tap the menu button in the top right corner of the app screen.
3. Select **Linked Users**.
4. All users and associated workspaces are listed.

## Link a new user and workspace

For more information about pairing users and workspaces, see User Setup.

1. Open the Fireblocks app on your mobile device.
2. Tap the menu button in the top right corner of the app screen.
3. Select **Linked Users**.
4. Tap the **+** symbol in the top right corner.
5. Verify your PIN code.
6. Scan the QR code presented in the Fireblocks Console.

## Remove a linked user

1. Open the Fireblocks app on your mobile device.
   - **Android**:
     1. Tap the menu button in the top right corner of the app screen.
     2. Select **Linked Users**.

<!-- page: 2 -->

   3. Tap the menu button to the right of the user you would like to remove.
   4. Select **Remove User**.
   5. Confirm your choice to remove the user. The user does not receive any notification.
   - **iOS**:
     1. Tap the menu button in the top right corner of the app screen.
     2. Tap **Linked Users**.
     3. Swipe left on the user you want to remove, then select **Delete**.
