        Compliance Integrations

             Note
             The AML and Travel Rule integrations are premium features that require additional
             purchases. Address Registry is a native Fireblocks feature available to all workspaces
             at no additional cost. Reach out to your Customer Success Manager to request
             access to the AML and Travel Rule integrations.


        Overview
        Fireblocks offers both native compliance checks and integrations with leading crypto
        compliance providers, so you can identify transaction counterparties and detect high-risk
        activity. In the Fireblocks Console, you manage these checks and configure policies for
        screening your transactions.
               Address registry: Fireblocks' built-in address registry enables you to establish
               deterministic identities for transaction counterparties. Unlike AML screening, the
               address registry does not assess risk, but rather identifies who is on the other side
               of a transaction. Learn more about Address registry.
               AML: Fireblocks integrates with Chainalysis and Elliptic to support automated Anti-
               Money Laundering (AML)/combating the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) compliance,
               as well as sanctions compliance operations. Learn more about the AML integration.
               Travel Rule: Fireblocks integrates with Notabene to analyze your inbound and
               outbound transaction activity and determine the additional data on the senders and
               recipients of your transactions you must provide for Travel Rule compliance. Learn
               more about the Travel Rule integration.
        These compliance functions operate at different stages of a transaction. AML screening
        assesses counterparty risk before a transaction proceeds. The address registry identifies
        counterparties deterministically. Travel Rule screening occurs after AML: if a counterparty is
        already flagged as high-risk, Travel Rule data exchange typically does not proceed. If you

        Enabling the Compliance tab




        The Compliance tab allows you to set up your AML and Travel Rule integrations. To enable
        the Compliance tab:
           1. Go to Settings > Compliance. Then select Get started.
           2. Submit a request to Fireblocks Support to register your interest in compliance
              integrations. Specify which providers and compliance types (AML, Travel Rule) you
              want to enable. Make sure to CC your Customer Success Manager in the request.
