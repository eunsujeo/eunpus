# Blockchains SLA

*Updated 1 year ago* (as of 2026-05-18 capture)

<!-- page: 1 -->

Fireblocks currently has three separate node types:

1. **Nodes hosted by Fireblocks.**
2. **Nodes hosted by certified vendors.**
3. **Nodes hosted by various blockchain foundations.**

Other than nodes we host, we currently support a few certified node vendors, which have undergone our vetting process and are considered trusted. For both our hosted nodes and these vendors' nodes, we are committed to a total uptime SLA of **99.9%**. For all other foundation-provided node vendors, we have no SLA since we cannot guarantee their blockchain's operational performance.
