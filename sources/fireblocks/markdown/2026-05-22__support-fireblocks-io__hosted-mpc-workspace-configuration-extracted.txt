        Hosted MPC Workspace
        Configuration
        Updated 1 year ago

        When you request to own and manage all of your MPC signing keys, Fireblocks creates a
        workspace with a “Hosted MPC” tag for you. The onboarding process requires careful
        planning of the Co-Signer’s deployment, so it is necessary to work closely with our
        customer support staff. Contact your Customer Success Manager to learn more about the
        process.

            Note:
            You must open and configure a new workspace for a Hosted MPC setup, as modifying an
            existing SaaS MPC workspace is impossible.
