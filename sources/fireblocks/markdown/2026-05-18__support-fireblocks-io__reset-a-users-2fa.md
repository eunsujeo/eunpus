# Reset a user's 2FA

*Updated 2 days ago* (as of 2026-05-18 capture)

<!-- page: 1 -->

Workspace Owners can reset another user's two-factor authentication (2FA) from the Fireblocks Console when the user loses access to their 2FA app. Resetting 2FA does not change the user's login email or password.

## Reset another user's 2FA

To reset a user's 2FA:

1. In the Fireblocks Console, go to **Settings > Users**.
2. Find the user, then select the more actions menu (⋮) > **Reset 2FA**.

The user receives an email confirming that their 2FA has been reset. They can then log in to the Fireblocks Console and reconfigure their 2FA app.

## Reset the workspace Owner's 2FA

The workspace Owner cannot reset their own 2FA through the Console. To reset the Owner's 2FA, contact Fireblocks Support.
