# Algorand blockchain limitations

*Updated 1 year ago* (as of 2026-05-18 capture) — **PLACEHOLDER**

## One-line summary

Algorand: a transaction ID must be signed within 1000-blocks (~50 minutes) of creation.

## Why this matters

Algorand의 짧은 signing window는 Owner/Admin Quorum approval 흐름에서 시간 제약을 만든다 (특히 mobile approval 지연 시).
