        Changing your AML policy

            Note
            The AML integration is a premium feature that requires an additional purchase. Reach
            out to your Customer Success Manager if you do not have an agreement with an AML
            provider or you need help enabling the feature.

          1. Go to Settings > Compliance, and select your AML provider.
          2. Select Change policy for the policy you want to change.
          3. Upload the .csv file with your new policy, and select Upload policy.
          4. Fireblocks Support will review your policy and reach out to you with any questions
             they have.
          5. Once approved, Fireblocks Support enables the new policy in your workspace and
             you can view it in your Console.
