        Travel Rule policy templates

             Note: This is a premium feature that requires additional purchase and enablement by
             Fireblocks Support. If you're interested in this feature for your workspace, contact
             your Customer Success Manager.


        Before you begin
        Read the Travel Rule Transaction Screening overview before configuring your policies. If
        you have questions, contact Fireblocks Support.



        Download and complete a template
        Download a template below and read the instructions on the Instructions tab. Enter your
        workspace name at the top of the sheet.
        When updating an existing policy, include all existing policy rules and the new rules in your
        file. Highlight any new or changed rules and ensure they're in the correct order.
        After completing the template, upload your policies to the Fireblocks Console.
